# Contributing

Veritas is a public receipt, attestation and verification library, not an authorization or execution layer.

Discussion, issues and verification scenarios are welcome proposal/evidence surfaces. They are not normative by themselves. Normative receipt/verification semantics change only through an accepted, versioned change under [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).

Contributions must be submitted through pull requests and include:

- problem statement and stable issue/proposal reference;
- proposed normative or non-normative change;
- compatibility class: major, minor or patch;
- security impact;
- affected contracts, schemas and verification behavior;
- required positive/negative verification cases where behavior changes;
- migration notes where applicable;
- external evidence/references where relevant;
- proposed target version.

Normative language uses MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT and MAY as defined by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174.

Do not submit private or internal source code, customer evidence, credentials, unpublished patent material, internal thresholds or proprietary evaluation logic.

A private implementation or experiment may motivate a public proposal, but implementation existence does not make the proposal normative.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be licensed under the MIT License and that you have the right to submit it.
